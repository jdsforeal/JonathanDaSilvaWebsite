Profile

Belgian cinematographer. Usually behind the camera.

Biography

Jonathan’s approach focuses on using light, composition and camera movement to support tone, performance and story, moving between controlled compositions and more instinctive camera work depending on the project.

Alongside his work as a cinematographer, he co-founded HEMERA in 2020, a production company created by technicians to support fiction, music videos and commissioned projects.
